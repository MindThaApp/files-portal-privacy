# Files Portal Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17


This policy covers Files Portal, published by MindtheApp.

Files Portal does not collect usage data, analytics or telemetry. It has no accounts and no advertising. The only information that can leave your PC for us is a crash report (on by default, and you can turn it off) or a feedback message you choose to send, as described below.

## Crash reports

When Files Portal crashes, it can send us a crash report: the type of error and its message, the list of program functions that were running (the stack trace), the Files Portal version and edition, the Windows and .NET versions and the language, the amount of memory, and the names and versions of the program components that were loaded. Before anything leaves your PC, file and folder paths are reduced to placeholders (for example `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\<file>.docx`), and your user name and your PC's name are removed. No files, screenshots or memory dumps are sent. Crash reports are on by default. The first-run welcome, and a one-time page after an update, show a "Send crash reports to help fix problems" checkbox that you can untick; you can also untick it at any time in Settings > General > Privacy > Crash reports. Unticking turns crash reporting off completely and deletes any report still waiting. If you press "Send report", the same kind of redacted information is sent together with the app's logs and settings, with all paths removed. Reports are processed for us by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) and stored in its EU data region (Germany). A random installation number groups reports from the same copy of the app; it cannot identify you. Reports are kept for the retention period of our Sentry plan and are then deleted automatically. Reports are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not sold or shared with anyone else. To ask about or delete a report, contact us and quote the reference shown after sending.

## Feedback

"Send feedback" (Settings > About, the command palette or the app menu) sends the message you write and the Files Portal version and edition, and nothing else unless you tick more: no installation number and no device details. You can add your e-mail address if you would like an answer, a screenshot of the Files Portal window you are using, and the same redacted diagnostics as "Send report"; each is off by default. File and folder paths and your user name are removed from the message before it is sent. A screenshot shows what is in the Files Portal window at that moment, which can include file and folder names, so only add one if you are happy to share what it shows. Your e-mail address is used only to reply to you. Feedback is something you send on purpose, so it works whatever you chose for crash reports, including Never. It is processed by Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) in its EU data region. If you are offline, or the message could not be delivered, it waits on your PC and is sent on a later start. Your e-mail address is only kept on your PC if you tick "Remember my e-mail". To ask about or delete a message, contact us and quote the reference shown after sending.

## Data that stays on your PC

Settings, layouts, tags, saved searches, history and caches are stored on your PC in `%APPDATA%\FilesPortal` and `%LOCALAPPDATA%\FilesPortal`. Passwords you save for network sites are kept in Windows Credential Manager. None of this is sent to us. Settings > Advanced > "Remove Files Portal data" deletes it.

## Updates and connections you make

The Microsoft Store version asks the Microsoft Store once a day whether an update is available; it never downloads or installs anything by itself. When you connect to a network share, an FTP or WebDAV site, a nearby device or a Git server, Files Portal talks only to the address you chose, and nothing from those connections is sent to us.

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be published at this address.
