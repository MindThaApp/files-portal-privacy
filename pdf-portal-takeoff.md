# PDF Portal Takeoff: privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-23 (version 0.9.2)

PDF Portal Takeoff is a quantity takeoff app for Windows, published by MindtheApp. It reads PDF drawings and helps you count, measure and price what is on them. This page explains what happens to your information.

## Your drawings stay on your PC

PDF Portal Takeoff opens your PDF drawings on your own computer, read-only. It never changes, moves or uploads them, and it has no servers that receive them. Counts, measurements and other takeoff work are done on your PC.

## Your projects

- A takeoff project (a `.takeoff` file) is saved where you choose. It stores references to your drawings (their location and a fingerprint to spot changes), not copies of them.
- When you save over a project, the app writes the new version first and checks it before replacing the old one. It keeps earlier versions as backups and automatic snapshots in the app's own data folder on your PC.
- Reports and exports are saved only where you choose, under a new name unless you confirm replacing a file.
- The name of the person who reviewed an item is stored only in the project file, never written into a PDF.

## No account, no tracking

There is no account or sign-in. The app has no advertising and no usage tracking.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, it can send a crash report so we can fix the problem.

- Crash reports are **on by default**. You are asked once when you first start the app, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings; turning them off stops reporting at once.
- A report contains the error and its call stack (the names of the parts of the program involved), the app version and your Windows version.
- A report **never contains your drawings** or anything from them: no PDF content, page images, text, labels, tags, item names or quantities. It also never contains file or folder names, paths, your Windows user name or your PC name. These are removed on your PC before anything is sent.
- No screenshots, attachments, usage sessions or performance data are sent.
- Reports go to our crash-reporting service, Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), in its EU data region, used only to fix crashes. The service sees your IP address as part of the connection, but **no IP address is stored**.
- Reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have a report deleted sooner, email us (see Contact).

## What is stored on your PC

- Your settings, including your crash-report choice.
- Project backups and automatic snapshots, in the app's own data folder.
- Uninstalling the app removes its data folder. Your drawings and the projects you saved elsewhere are not touched.

## Purchases and the Microsoft Store

Paid features are bought through the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows whether you own them. We receive no payment details and no personal information from the purchase.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
