# PDF Portal Takeoff Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-23 (version 0.9.2)

This policy covers PDF Portal Takeoff, published by MindtheApp. PDF Portal Takeoff is a quantity takeoff app for Windows: it opens PDF drawings and helps you count, measure and review what is on them.

PDF Portal Takeoff does not collect usage data or analytics. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no usage tracking. Your drawings are never sent to us. The only thing that can reach us is a crash report, which is on by default and which you can turn off with one click (see "Crash reports" below).

## Your drawings stay on your PC

PDF Portal Takeoff opens your PDF drawings on your own computer, read-only. It never changes, moves, renames or uploads them, and we have no servers that receive them. Symbol counts, measurements, review decisions and quantities are all worked out on your PC and stay there.

## Data that stays on your PC

- Your crash-report choice, in a small settings file.
- A crash report that could not be sent yet (see "Waiting reports" below).

This data is stored in the app's own data folder on your PC (`PortalTakeoff` under your local app data). None of it is sent to us, apart from crash reports if you leave them on. Uninstalling the app removes this folder. Your drawings are not touched.

## Crash reports

**On by default.** When the app runs into an error or crashes, it sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem quickly.

**How to turn them off.** The first time you start the app, it asks "Send crash reports?" with "Keep on" and "Turn off". You can change your choice at any time with the Settings button in the app, under "Send crash reports (no drawing content)". Turning reports off takes effect at once: nothing more is sent, and any reports still waiting on your PC are deleted.

**What's sent.** A crash report contains only:

- the type of error and where in the app's code it happened (the code's method and file names, with folder names removed);
- the error message, after file names, folder paths, your Windows user name and your PC name have been replaced with placeholders, cut to 300 characters;
- which part of the app reported it, and a short list of the app's own fixed steps before it (such as "app started"), never anything you typed or opened;
- the app version and channel (Store, beta or development build), a random ID for the report and the time;
- the Windows version, the .NET version and basic hardware details (processor type and number of processors, and amount of memory).

**What's never sent.** A crash report never contains your drawings or anything from them: no PDF content, page images, text, labels, tags, item names, counts or quantities. Any extra data attached to an error is dropped, because it could hold text from a drawing. A report also never contains file names, folder paths (paths include your Windows user name, so they are removed), your Windows account name, your PC name, your time zone, an installation ID, your IP address, logs, settings, attachments or screenshots. The app sends no usage sessions and no performance data. If the app cannot be sure a report is clean, the report is not sent.

**Where it goes.** Crash reports go to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), in its EU data region, in a project used only for PDF Portal Takeoff. They are used only to fix problems. Like any internet connection, the service sees your IP address while a report is delivered, but storing IP addresses is turned off for our whole account, so no IP address is kept with a report.

**Waiting reports.** A report that cannot be sent yet (for example while you are offline) waits in the `CrashReports` folder in the app's data folder and is sent the next time the app starts. Turning crash reports off deletes it.

**How long it is kept.** Crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days. To have a report deleted sooner, email us (see Contact).

Reports are not linked to your identity, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are not sold or shared with anyone else.

## Purchases and the Microsoft Store

PDF Portal Takeoff is free to download. Paid features are one-time purchases handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows whether you own them. We receive no payment details and no personal information from a purchase.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
