# Files Portal privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Files Portal is a file manager for Windows, made by MindtheApp. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information. The short version: it does not collect any.

## What we collect

Nothing. Files Portal has no accounts and no analytics. It does not send usage data, crash reports, file names, folder names or anything else about you or your computer to us or to anyone else. There are no advertising or tracking libraries inside the app.

## What stays on your PC

Files Portal keeps a few things on your own computer so it can work the way you expect:

- Your settings and preferences, such as theme, layout and favourites.
- Your tags and the list of recent places and files shown on the Home page.
- Crash logs. If the app ever crashes, a small log is written to the app's own data folder on your PC to help you or a helper understand what happened. These logs are never sent anywhere. You can delete them at any time by deleting the app's data folder.

## Your files

Files Portal reads and changes files only when you ask it to, for example when you copy, move, rename, delete, preview or open one. Your files are not uploaded or shared by the app on its own.

## Sharing to nearby devices

If you choose to share a file with another device, Files Portal can use the open LocalSend protocol and a QR code to hand the file over directly on your local network (your home or office Wi-Fi or network). The transfer goes straight between the two devices. It does not pass through any server of ours or any server on the internet, and nothing is kept after the transfer. Receiving files from other devices is off until you turn it on, and you are asked before each incoming transfer is accepted.

## Cloud storage (OneDrive, Google Drive)

Files Portal does not sign in to any cloud service and does not use any cloud account or key. It only looks at the sync folders that the OneDrive or Google Drive app has already put on your PC, the same way it looks at any other folder. Whatever those apps upload or download is handled by them, under their own privacy policies.

## Children

Files Portal does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
