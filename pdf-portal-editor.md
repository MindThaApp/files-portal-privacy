# PDF Portal Editor Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17

This policy covers PDF Portal Editor, published by MindtheApp.

PDF Portal Editor does not collect usage data, analytics or telemetry. It has no accounts, no advertising and no crash reporting. Nothing about you or your documents is sent to us.

## Your documents stay on your PC

PDF Portal Editor opens, reads, edits and saves your files on your own computer. It never uploads your documents, and we have no servers that receive them. Tools that produce a result save it as a file on your PC, by default under a new name so your original is kept.

## Data that stays on your PC

- Your settings and the list of recently opened PDFs (file names and the page you stopped at), so you can pick up where you left off.
- Your saved signatures, stamps and other items you create in the app.
- The start date of the Pro features trial, in a small file in the app's data folder.
- If something goes wrong, a short error note in a log file in the app's data folder. It is never sent anywhere.

This data is stored in the app's own data folder on your PC. None of it is sent to us, and uninstalling the app removes it.

## Pro features and the Microsoft Store

Pro features is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows whether you own it. We receive no payment details and no personal information from the purchase.

## Links, read aloud and text recognition

- A link inside a PDF is only opened after you confirm, in your default browser or mail app; that app then handles the request.
- Read aloud uses the speech voices installed in Windows.
- Text recognition (OCR) runs on your PC. Windows may download its own recognition files the first time it is used.
- Dictation buttons start Voice Portal, a separate app, if you have it installed. They send nothing themselves.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
